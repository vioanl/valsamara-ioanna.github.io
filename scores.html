Scores

Open scores for ensembles.

Brave New Sounds (TMM66 in the conflict for the river that only flows one way)

2025 / system score for group improvisation

Adapted from a reactive robot architecture. The piece is not time-dependent: it runs as a continuous loop of listening, mapping, planning, deciding and playing. The ensemble agrees on a total duration and stops when that time is reached. The score documents possible behaviours rather than fixed notes.

Inevitable Cosmos (chaos as a percentage)

2024 / three-part system and graphic score for 3–7 performers

The ensemble sets the total duration. Parts I, II and III last 5%, 27% and 68% of it, echoing the proportions of visible matter, dark matter and dark energy in the universe. Material is read from circular staves, with rules that shift from part to part, so the form stays open but guided.

In A Room (a body, a sentiment, a room of one’s own)

2024 / score for group, voice, objects and electronics

Players work within limits and use existing recordings from outer space as material, placing that sound quietly in the room. The group listens to the space and answers it, so the music grows from the room itself. Before playing, the ensemble writes its own timeline of who plays where and when inside the agreed time.

66 ways to look above (The sky isn’t silent)

2024 / system and graphic score for group

Each page is a two-dimensional diagram that the performers map to sound together: the vertical axis is pitch, the horizontal axis is time in 19 intervals, and the size of a shape is its intensity. Colours assign parts to players; diagrams with several colours are played collaboratively.

66 ways to walk the Earth (paths)

2023 / system and graphic score for three performers

Built from the same rules as its sister piece: pitch rises up the page, time is divided into 19 units whose length the trio chooses, and larger shapes are louder. Each performer takes one colour, white, red or black. Before playing, the trio agrees the order of pages, the mappings and the roles, so no one leads. Pages are never repeated, so every realization stays unique.

Curved Soundtime (when massive sounds fall into a flat four-dimensional continuum)

2023 / system and graphic score for six performers or six groups, 43 pages

Curvature from general relativity, translated into music. Each page shows six lines and each player or group takes one. A flat line is silence; a line bends in proportion to its “sound mass”, the sound level multiplied by the number of simultaneous sounds. To bend it further, play louder or add more sounds. The ensemble decides how long each page lasts, and the piece can be read from the first page to the last as one arc.

TOPOS 66

2023 / three scores for group, concert or installation

Three worlds: two suns and cosmic radiation, with an optional SuperCollider drone; neural signal relays built from listening to a recording; and a photographic soundscape performed collectively. In concert, any of the three can be played alone. As an installation, all three are realized together.

Delimitations (or floating above and inside the era of suspend disbelief)

2022 / 30 graphic scores for open instrumentation

Thirty self-contained prompts for group improvisation rather than movements of one piece. Performers talk together about how to translate the graphics into sound, reach a shared understanding and make a collective realization, not one person’s decision. A recorded realization is on Bandcamp.

Garden

2022 / participatory text score for group

The group becomes the garden. They listen, talk about what the flowers hear, play and record those sounds, then listen back while playing again: a loop of listening, sounding and reflecting.
