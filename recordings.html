Concept, text, cover art, electronics, MicroFreak and vocals by Ioanna Valsamara.

“call of the void” describes the impulse to hurl yourself into a void. While unnerving, it has nothing to do with suicidal ideation.

Full of space and dust,
changed within me also the rhythm of the world.
Noise jitters as rhythm, rhythm jitters as noise,
eating all the flowers with my teeth.
Whispers for the orange trees,
all this time, the wind was tearing up the sky.
Flesh and neuralgia,
when the night cracked as a lightning storm.

An aural project built on a collage poem whose lines are drawn from excerpts of various literary works; the order of the lines follows the sonic character of the tracks. Every track except the eighth was made remotely and carries one line of the poem, yet its spectromorphology holds impressions of the whole poem, the way a hologram does. Lo-fi recordings meet high-fidelity digital software. Instead of slow, minimal processes, the music takes an ADHD attitude of fast changes and more conventional structural and melodic gestures, open to noisier genres and to the awkwardness of improvised melody. The last track is a live smartphone recording, a direct tribute to free improvisation.

A recorded realization of Delimitations, the collection of 30 graphic scores for open instrumentation. The scores are available on the blog.
