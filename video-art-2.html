245sounds

2026 / video art with music

132 years after the landing on a new planet, the world is empty and silent. A man walks out searching for something to fill it, even one sound. Inside a white wound quarried into the mountain, a hole opens onto the planet’s untouched world, and 245 sounds never heard before are gathered. Then the mining of the new world begins. The music is built around a narrated text, given below in English.

Narration

It happened when they said it was 132 years after the landing on the planet.
It was 133 since the destruction of Gaia.
The world around was empty.

He was out in the outer world, listening.
One morning, in the hour between morning and noon, he felt he had to walk.
And to search for something.

He walked slowly.
The woman lived in a house that almost looked like a hut, with red walls and white-framed windows that reflected the light the sky brought. She saw him from the mountain. She had come out of the house and had been there for hours, waiting for the first sun to set. But that sun had only just risen into the sky and was burning the horizon.

Back then they still had no lights to light the nights, and they had not yet rediscovered electricity and the sciences.
She stayed there on the mountain and waited for hours. And nothing happened. Until, at some point, she saw his figure searching for something. What can anyone search for in nothing, she wondered. But he was searching.

That morning he wanted to find something else.
The world was empty of everything.
He was looking for something to fill it.
He wanted to find even one sound to be heard in the nothing.

When I saw him searching, I thought he knew more. How he knew why, in the new world, the sun sets eight times and why the stars so often vanish from the sky. Why the day lasts longer and the night longer still. How he would know why we are the only ones who move and run. How he knew what brought the endless quiet.
Or rather, how he knew what brought us here, to a place foreign and quiet, where nothing that sounds is ours.
But in the end he did not know.
Because whoever knows probably does not search.
And here we all search and no one finds, and we are all lost in a world that was not made for us.
In the end, I found it.
It was the hour between the third and the fourth sunset, and for my testimony to be clear I must say that on that day it was hotter than on the others, and the air reached my chest burnt and smelled of fire, as if I suddenly remembered the word fire, and its colour, and every time I had seen it or heard of it.
There, then, we had mutilated the mountain.
We had opened a wound, white and enormous,
to take whatever this new stone had to give us.
But in the depths I saw
a hole,
and that is where I went in.
And there is where I finally found the new world of the planet. And from this finding, 245 new sounds were gathered that we had never heard before.
Because there, humans had not yet arrived to take.
And so we began the mining of the new world.
And the sound grew and grew.

Text translated from the Greek.
