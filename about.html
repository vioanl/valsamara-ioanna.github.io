About

Ioanna Valsamara composes and performs experimental music with the 6daEXIt Improvisation Ensemble and the duo call of the void. Her practice combines voice, electronics and the Arturia MicroFreak with graphic and algorithmic scores, live coding and text. Her artistic interests centre on ecology and oppression.

She studied physics at the University of Ioannina and holds an MSc in Advanced Computer and Communication Systems from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, specialising in intelligent systems, with a thesis on speech emotion recognition. Those fields run through the scores: curvature from general relativity in Curved Soundtime, the matter and energy budget of the universe in Inevitable Cosmos, a reactive robot architecture in Brave New Sounds.
